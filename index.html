How It Works

1. Buyer finds item - Phones, cars, laptops, clothes, anything.

2. Buyer pays into escrow - Money is held safely by Mukambo.

3. Seller delivers - Seller sends or meets buyer.

4. Buyer confirms - Money is released to seller. Done!
